| # | Topic                                        | What to fix / develop                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| - | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | **Core axioms & terminology**                | Separate original 2001 concepts from later generalizations, explicitly define GMVA terms such as *Function, Refining, Income, Purchase Costs, Profit/Loss,* and *Summarizer*, and eliminate terminology drift such as CED → DEC.                      |
| 2 | **Dual hierarchy**                           | Formalize the distinction between the **Function structure** (“who performs economic activity”) and **Product Build Scope** (“what is being built”), connected by participation rather than merged into one hierarchy.                                |
| 3 | **Economic allocation model**                | Develop rigorous rules for allocating Function economics to Product Build Scope instances; the current example mainly demonstrates simple division by instance count.                                                                                 |
| 4 | **Value addition / Refining**                | Give *Refining* a testable economic definition: distinguish expenditure that merely consumes resources from expenditure that actually enables increased value, capability, price, volume or future contribution.                                      |
| 5 | **Causality & cross-function effects**       | Model how a negative Function can enable positive results elsewhere, while clearly separating demonstrated economic relationships from merely assumed causal effects—the 2001–03 scenarios do not prove causality.                                    |
| 6 | **Mathematical/computational specification** | Turn the recursive **Function → Function summaries → Organization** concept into precise equations, aggregation rules, data structures and a no-double-counting computational specification.                                                          |
| 7 | **Cross-organization testing**               | Apply the model to structurally different real organizations and compare whether the same concepts—Functions, scope, Refining and economic summarization—remain usable beyond the original hosting example.                                           |
| 8 | **Literature & novelty positioning**         | Explicitly compare GMVA with existing economic/management concepts and identify which parts are genuinely new—especially the independent dual hierarchy and recursive economic summarization—rather than claiming novelty for established components. |
